# English style pack

Applies the universal rules in `coaching.md` to talks given in English. Extend it as real decks teach you more.

## Endings and confidence

| Rule | Trips | Lands |
|---|---|---|
| End statements firmly; do not trail off with "so, yeah" or rising "...right?" | "It kind of helps, so, yeah." | "It helps parents prepare in half the time." |
| Drop hedges that undersell | "We tried to build", "We're hoping to" | "We built", "We will" |
| Frame effects as help, not guarantees | "Children become independent." | "It helps children do the routine on their own." |
| Avoid unfinished-sounding words when the fact is solid | "It's still under development" | "Three plans are available" |
| Avoid weak-sounding words | "There was a small bug, we fixed it" | "We improved it after the trial" |

## Length and flow

| Rule | Trips | Lands |
|---|---|---|
| One idea per sentence; start the turn with "But" | "Interest is growing but parents still..." | "Interest is growing. But parents still..." |
| Verb near the front | "The cards that are generated by the engine from what the parent said are shown" | "The engine turns what the parent said into cards." |
| Sense-matching verbs | "watch the voice" | "hear the voice" |

## Fillers

Cut "basically", "actually", "like", "you know", "kind of", "sort of", "um", unless the user keeps one on purpose (listed under `## Voice` in `memory.md`).
